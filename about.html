Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited is a Pakistan Stock Exchange licensed brokerage house and a Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate holder, operating under the regulatory oversight of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Company Overview

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”) is engaged in the business of securities brokerage at the Pakistan Stock Exchange. The Company provides brokerage services strictly in accordance with the rules, regulations, and directives issued by the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX), the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), and other applicable laws of Pakistan.

Regulatory Status

  • Licensed Brokerage House of the Pakistan Stock Exchange
  • Holder of a valid Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate
  • Regulated and supervised by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan

The Company conducts its operations in compliance with securities laws, PSX rules, SECP regulations, AML/CFT requirements, and internal compliance and risk management policies.

Nature of Services

The Company provides equity brokerage services to individual and institutional clients at the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Services are offered subject to account opening approval, regulatory compliance, and internal policies.

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited does not offer guaranteed returns, assured profit schemes, or discretionary portfolio management services unless separately authorized and permitted under applicable laws and regulatory approvals.

Corporate Governance & Compliance

The Company maintains internal governance, compliance, and control frameworks designed to ensure transparency, investor protection, and adherence to regulatory requirements.

The Company has adopted policies covering, inter alia:

  • Risk Management
  • Conflict of Interest
  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter Terrorist Financing (CTF)
  • Investor Complaint Handling & Grievance Redressal
  • Client Due Diligence and KYC

Client Focus & Investor Protection

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited is committed to acting honestly, fairly, and professionally in the best interests of its clients. Investor awareness, transparency, and protection are core principles of the Company’s business philosophy.
