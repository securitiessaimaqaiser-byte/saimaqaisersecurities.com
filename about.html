About Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is a licensed brokerage house operating under the regulatory framework of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

The company provides capital market and brokerage services with a strong focus on compliance, transparency, and long-term client relationships.

Vision & Mission

Our vision is to deliver ethical, transparent, and client-focused financial services while contributing positively to Pakistan’s capital markets.

Management

  • Strict adherence to SECP regulations
  • Client confidentiality and data protection
  • Ethical market practices
  • Transparent reporting and disclosures
