About Us

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”) is engaged in securities brokerage at the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX). The Company operates strictly in accordance with PSX rules, SECP regulations, and applicable laws of Pakistan.

Regulatory Status

  • Licensed Brokerage House of Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX)
  • Holder of a valid Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate
  • Regulated and supervised by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)

The Company conducts its operations in compliance with securities laws, PSX rules, SECP regulations, AML/CFT requirements, and internal compliance and risk management policies.

Nature of Services

The Company provides equity brokerage services to individual and institutional clients at the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Services are offered subject to regulatory approvals, account opening verification, and internal compliance review.

The Company does not offer guaranteed returns, assured profit schemes, or discretionary portfolio management services unless separately authorized under applicable laws and regulatory approvals.
