About Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is a licensed brokerage house operating under the regulatory framework of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

The company provides capital market and brokerage services with a strong focus on compliance, transparency, and long-term client relationships.

Vision & Mission

To provide transparent, compliant, and client-focused capital market services while upholding the highest standards of integrity and professionalism.

Management

The company is managed by experienced professionals with deep expertise in Pakistan’s capital markets and regulatory environment.

Our Legal Facilitation

All operations are conducted in strict compliance with SECP regulations and applicable laws governing brokerage and capital market activities.
