About Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is a licensed brokerage house operating under the regulatory framework of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP). The company provides capital market services with a strong emphasis on transparency, compliance, and investor protection.

Corporate Profile

The company is engaged in brokerage and related financial services in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations governing the capital markets of Pakistan. Operations are conducted in line with SECP and Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) requirements.

Regulatory Status

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is duly registered and regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. The firm maintains internal compliance, risk management, and control frameworks to ensure regulatory adherence.

Business Principles

The company operates on principles of professionalism, integrity, and ethical conduct. Client interests are prioritized, and services are delivered with due diligence and responsibility.

Risk Awareness

Investment in securities involves market risks. Clients are advised to review all relevant disclosures and seek independent financial advice before making investment decisions.
