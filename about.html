Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited is a Pakistan Stock Exchange licensed brokerage house and Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate holder, regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Company Overview

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”) is engaged in the business of securities brokerage at the Pakistan Stock Exchange. The Company operates strictly in accordance with the rules, regulations, and directives issued by the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Regulatory Status

The Company is:

  • A licensed broker of the Pakistan Stock Exchange
  • A holder of a valid Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate
  • Regulated and supervised by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan

The Company conducts its business in compliance with applicable securities laws, PSX rules, SECP regulations, and internal compliance policies.

Our Services

The Company provides equity brokerage services to its clients at the Pakistan Stock Exchange. Services are offered subject to regulatory approvals and internal policies.

The Company does not provide investment guarantees, profit assurances, or discretionary portfolio management unless separately authorized and permitted under applicable regulations.

Corporate Governance & Compliance

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited maintains internal control systems, compliance mechanisms, and supervisory structures designed to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements and ethical business practices.

The Company has adopted policies covering:

  • Risk management
  • Conflict of interest
  • Anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF)
  • Investor complaint handling and grievance redressal

Client Focus

The Company is committed to dealing with clients fairly, honestly, and professionally. Investor protection and transparency form a core part of the Company’s business philosophy.
