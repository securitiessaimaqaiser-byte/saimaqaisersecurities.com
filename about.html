About Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is a licensed brokerage house operating under the regulatory framework of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP). The company is engaged in providing capital market services with a focus on transparency, regulatory compliance, and client protection.

Corporate Profile

The company offers brokerage and related services in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations governing the capital markets of Pakistan. All operations are conducted with strict adherence to SECP guidelines and Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) regulations.

Regulatory Status

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is duly registered and regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. The company maintains internal controls, compliance procedures, and risk management practices to ensure the integrity of its operations.

Business Principles

The company operates on principles of fairness, professionalism, and ethical conduct. Client interests are placed at the forefront, and all services are delivered with due care and responsibility.

Risk Awareness

Investment in securities involves market risks. Clients are encouraged to review all relevant disclosures and seek independent financial advice before engaging in investment activities.
