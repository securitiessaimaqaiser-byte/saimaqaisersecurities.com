Account Opening Form

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited welcomes investors to open trading accounts in accordance with applicable laws, SECP regulations, and internal compliance requirements.

How to Open an Account

To open an account, prospective clients are required to complete the prescribed account opening form and submit the necessary supporting documentation.

  • Duly filled account opening form
  • Copy of valid CNIC / NICOP / Passport
  • Proof of bank account
  • Any additional documents required under KYC / CDD regulations

Download Forms

The account opening form and related documents can be obtained by contacting our office or will be made available for download as required.

For assistance, please reach out to our customer support team using the contact details provided on the website.
