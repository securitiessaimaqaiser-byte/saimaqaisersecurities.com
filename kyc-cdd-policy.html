KYC / CDD Policy

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is committed to preventing money laundering, terrorist financing, and other illicit financial activities by implementing robust Know Your Customer (KYC) and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) procedures in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Purpose of KYC / CDD

The purpose of KYC and CDD measures is to verify the identity of clients, understand the nature of their business relationship with the company, and assess potential risks associated with such relationships.

  • Verification of customer identity and documentation
  • Understanding source of funds and nature of transactions
  • Ongoing monitoring of client activity
  • Risk-based customer classification

Regulatory Compliance

The company follows KYC and CDD requirements as prescribed under the Anti-Money Laundering Act, applicable SECP regulations, and guidelines issued by relevant regulatory authorities.

Enhanced due diligence measures may be applied where higher risk is identified, and records are maintained for the period required by law.
