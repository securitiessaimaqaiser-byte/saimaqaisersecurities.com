Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited is committed to identifying, preventing, managing, and disclosing conflicts of interest in a fair and transparent manner, in accordance with the rules of the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Company Information

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”) is a Pakistan Stock Exchange licensed brokerage house and Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate holder, regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

What is a Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest arises when the personal interests of the Company, its directors, officers, employees, or associated persons may interfere, or appear to interfere, with the best interests of clients or investors.

Types of Potential Conflicts

  • Proprietary trading by the Company or related parties
  • Personal trading by employees or associated persons
  • Commission-based remuneration structures
  • Receipt of non-monetary benefits or incentives
  • Relationships with listed companies or other market participants
  • Allocation of trades among clients

Management of Conflicts

The Company has implemented internal policies and procedures to identify and manage conflicts of interest, including:

  • Segregation of duties and functional responsibilities
  • Internal controls and supervisory mechanisms
  • Disclosure of conflicts where required
  • Restrictions on personal trading by employees
  • Compliance monitoring and periodic reviews

Disclosure to Clients

Where a material conflict of interest cannot be effectively managed, the Company will disclose such conflict to the affected clients in a clear and timely manner, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Client Interest Priority

The Company is committed to acting honestly, fairly, and professionally in the best interests of its clients. Client interests are given priority over the Company’s own interests wherever required by law or regulation.

Employee Responsibilities

Directors, officers, and employees are required to:

  • Avoid situations that may give rise to conflicts of interest
  • Disclose any actual or potential conflicts promptly
  • Comply with internal policies and regulatory requirements

Policy Review

This Conflict of Interest Policy is reviewed periodically and may be updated to reflect changes in applicable laws, regulations, or business practices.

Regulatory Disclaimer
This policy is issued in compliance with the rules and regulations of the Pakistan Stock Exchange and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. In case of any inconsistency, applicable laws and regulatory requirements shall prevail.
