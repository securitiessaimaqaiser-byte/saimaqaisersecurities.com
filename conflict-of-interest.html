Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”) is committed to identifying, preventing, managing, and disclosing conflicts of interest in a fair and transparent manner, in accordance with the rules of the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Applicability

This Policy applies to the Company’s directors, officers, employees, authorized persons, associated persons, and any other individuals acting on behalf of the Company. Compliance with this Policy is mandatory and forms part of the Company’s governance framework.

Company Information

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited is a Pakistan Stock Exchange licensed brokerage house and Trading Right Entitlement (TRE) Certificate holder, regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

What is a Conflict of Interest

A conflict of interest arises when the personal, financial, or other interests of the Company or its associated persons conflict, or appear to conflict, with the duties owed to clients, investors, or the market.

Types of Potential Conflicts

  • Proprietary trading by the Company or related parties
  • Personal trading by directors, officers, or employees
  • Commission-based remuneration or incentive structures
  • Receipt of gifts, benefits, or inducements
  • Business or personal relationships with listed companies
  • Allocation of trades or investment opportunities among clients

Management of Conflicts

The Company has implemented internal policies and procedures to effectively identify and manage conflicts of interest, including:

  • Segregation of duties and functional responsibilities
  • Internal controls and supervisory oversight
  • Restrictions on personal and proprietary trading
  • Mandatory disclosure of conflicts where applicable
  • Compliance monitoring and periodic internal reviews

Disclosure to Clients

Where a material conflict of interest cannot be adequately managed, the Company shall disclose such conflict to affected clients in a clear, timely, and transparent manner, in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Client Interest Priority

The Company is committed to acting honestly, fairly, and professionally in the best interests of its clients. Client interests shall take precedence over the Company’s own interests where required by law or regulation.

Responsibilities of Directors & Employees

  • Avoid situations that may give rise to conflicts of interest
  • Promptly disclose any actual or potential conflicts
  • Comply with internal policies, codes of conduct, and regulatory requirements

Client Awareness

This Policy is made available on the Company’s website for the information of clients and investors. By maintaining a relationship with the Company, clients are deemed to have been informed of this Policy.

Policy Review

This Conflict of Interest Policy is reviewed periodically and may be updated to reflect changes in applicable laws, regulations, or business practices.

Regulatory Supremacy
This Policy is issued in compliance with the rules and regulations of the Pakistan Stock Exchange and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan. In case of any inconsistency, applicable laws and regulatory requirements shall prevail.
