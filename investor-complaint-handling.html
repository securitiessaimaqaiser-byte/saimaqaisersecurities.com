Purpose of This Policy
This Investor Complaint Handling & Grievance Redressal Policy is issued in accordance with the requirements of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX), and applicable laws. The objective is to ensure fair, transparent, and timely resolution of investor complaints.

Company Information

Company Name: Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited
Regulatory Status: PSX Licensed Brokerage House (TRE Holder)
Regulator: Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)

How to Lodge a Complaint

Investors may lodge complaints related to trading, settlement, account maintenance, or any other service provided by Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited using the following channels:

  • Email: compliance@saimaqaisersecurities.com
  • Written complaint addressed to the Compliance Department
  • In-person submission at the registered office

Complaints should include relevant details such as client code, CNIC, contact information, and supporting documents (if any).

Internal Complaint Handling Process

  • All complaints are recorded and acknowledged upon receipt.
  • The Compliance Department conducts an internal review.
  • Relevant departments are consulted where necessary.
  • A written response is provided to the investor.

The Company aims to resolve complaints within seven (7) working days, subject to the nature and complexity of the complaint.

Escalation to SECP

If an investor is not satisfied with the resolution provided by Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited, or if the complaint is not resolved within the prescribed timeframe, the investor may escalate the matter to the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Investors may lodge complaints with SECP through its official complaint management system, in accordance with SECP procedures.

No Retaliation Policy

Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited strictly prohibits any form of retaliation against investors who lodge complaints in good faith. Filing a complaint will not affect the investor’s relationship or services with the Company.

Record Keeping

All investor complaints and related correspondence are documented and retained in accordance with SECP record-keeping requirements and applicable laws.

Regulatory Disclaimer
This policy is subject to change in accordance with amendments in applicable laws, rules, and regulations issued by SECP or PSX from time to time.
