Purpose of This Policy
This Investor Complaint Handling & Grievance Redressal Policy is issued in accordance with the requirements of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP), Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX), and applicable laws. The objective is to ensure fair, transparent, and timely resolution of investor complaints.

Company Information

Company Name: Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited
Regulatory Status: Pakistan Stock Exchange Licensed Brokerage House (TRE Holder)
Regulator: Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP)

How to Lodge a Complaint

Investors may lodge complaints relating to trading, settlement, account maintenance, or any other service provided by the Company through the following channels:

  • Email: compliance@saimaqaisersecurities.com
  • Written complaint addressed to the Compliance Department
  • In-person submission at the Company’s registered office

Complaints should include client code, CNIC, contact details, description of the issue, and supporting documents (if any). Complaints may be submitted in English or Urdu and are processed free of cost.

Internal Complaint Handling Process

  • All complaints are formally recorded upon receipt.
  • Complaints are acknowledged within one (1) working day.
  • The Compliance Department conducts an internal investigation.
  • Relevant departments are consulted where required.
  • A written response is communicated to the investor.

The Company aims to resolve complaints within seven (7) working days, subject to the nature and complexity of the complaint.

The Compliance Officer is responsible for oversight, monitoring, and reporting of the complaint handling process.

Escalation to PSX & SECP

If an investor is not satisfied with the resolution provided by Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited, the investor may escalate the complaint to the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) in accordance with PSX complaint handling procedures.

If the matter remains unresolved, investors may further escalate the complaint to the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) through its official complaint management system.

Details of PSX and SECP complaint mechanisms are available on their respective official websites.

No Retaliation Policy

The Company strictly prohibits retaliation against investors who lodge complaints in good faith. Filing a complaint will not adversely affect the investor’s relationship or services with the Company.

Record Keeping

All complaints, investigations, and correspondence are documented and retained in accordance with SECP record-keeping requirements, PSX rules, and applicable laws of Pakistan.
