Company Overview

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is a licensed brokerage house operating under the regulatory framework of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP). The company provides brokerage and capital market services with a strong emphasis on compliance, transparency, and investor protection.

The firm conducts its operations in accordance with applicable laws, rules, and regulations, and maintains internal controls to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements.

Core Principles

  • Regulatory compliance and ethical conduct
  • Transparency in operations and disclosures
  • Client protection and risk awareness
  • Professional and responsible service delivery

Regulatory Status

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan and operates in compliance with the rules and regulations of the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX).

Clients are advised to review all relevant disclosures and risk statements before engaging in investment activities.
