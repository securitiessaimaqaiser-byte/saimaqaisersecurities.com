General Investment Risk

Trading in equity securities involves the risk of loss. Prices of securities may fluctuate due to market conditions, economic factors, company performance, political developments, and other variables. Investors may lose part or all of their invested capital.

Market Risk

Market risk arises from changes in market prices, liquidity, and volatility. Sudden market movements may result in losses and may affect the ability to execute trades at desired prices.

Liquidity Risk

Certain securities may have limited trading volumes. In such cases, investors may face difficulty buying or selling securities at prevailing market prices, which may result in losses.

Operational & System Risk

Trading systems, communication networks, and electronic platforms may experience disruptions, delays, or failures due to technical, mechanical, or external factors. Such events may affect order execution or access to trading facilities.

Regulatory Risk

Changes in laws, regulations, taxation policies, or regulatory frameworks may affect securities markets, trading conditions, or investor rights. Compliance with new requirements may impact investment outcomes.
