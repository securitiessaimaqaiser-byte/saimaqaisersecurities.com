Investing in securities involves risk. Before making any investment decision, investors should carefully consider their financial situation, investment objectives, and risk tolerance.

Market Risk

The value of investments may fluctuate due to market conditions, economic factors, political events, or changes in regulations. Prices of securities can go up or down and investors may incur losses.

Liquidity Risk

Certain securities may not be readily tradable due to low market liquidity. This may result in difficulty buying or selling such securities at desired prices.

Credit Risk

Investments in debt instruments are subject to the risk that the issuer may fail to meet its financial obligations.

Volatility Risk

Capital markets can be volatile. Sudden price movements may occur due to unforeseen events, leading to potential losses.
