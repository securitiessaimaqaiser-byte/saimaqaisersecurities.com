Risk Disclosure Statement

This Risk Disclosure Statement is provided by Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited in compliance with applicable regulatory requirements. Clients and investors are advised to carefully read and understand the risks associated with trading and investment in securities.

Market Risk

Securities markets are subject to fluctuations arising from various factors including economic conditions, political developments, interest rates, and market sentiment. Prices of securities may move adversely and result in losses.

Liquidity Risk

Certain securities may have limited liquidity, which may make it difficult to buy or sell such securities at desired prices or within a desired timeframe.

Credit Risk

Credit risk refers to the possibility that a counterparty may fail to meet its obligations, resulting in financial loss to the investor.

Operational Risk

Operational risks may arise from system failures, human error, or external events, which could affect the execution or settlement of transactions.

No Guarantee of Returns

Past performance of securities or investment strategies is not indicative of future results. There is no guarantee of profits, and investors may lose part or all of their invested capital.

Client Responsibility

Investors are responsible for understanding the nature of transactions they enter into and for assessing whether such transactions are appropriate in light of their financial circumstances and investment objectives.
