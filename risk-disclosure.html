Risk Disclosure

Investment in capital markets involves a high degree of risk. Prices of securities may fluctuate and can result in loss of principal invested.

Clients should carefully assess their financial situation, investment objectives, and risk tolerance before engaging in trading or investment activities.

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited does not provide any assurance or guarantee of returns. All investment decisions are made at the client’s own discretion and risk.

Clients are encouraged to seek independent financial advice where necessary and to read all relevant disclosure documents before investing.
