Risk Disclosure

Investing in securities involves risk. This Risk Disclosure Statement is intended to inform investors of the potential risks associated with investing in financial instruments and markets.

Market Risk

The value of securities may fluctuate due to changes in market conditions, economic factors, political events, interest rates, or other variables. Investors may experience losses, including the loss of principal invested.

Volatility Risk

Prices of securities can be volatile and may change rapidly over short periods of time. Such volatility may result in significant gains or losses.

Liquidity Risk

Some securities may not be easily sold or converted into cash without a substantial loss in value. Market liquidity may vary depending on market conditions.

Credit Risk

Credit risk refers to the possibility that an issuer or counterparty may fail to meet its financial obligations, resulting in a loss to the investor.

Operational & Technical Risk

Trading systems, communication networks, or technical infrastructure may experience delays, disruptions, or failures that could affect the execution of trades or access to information.

Regulatory Risk

Changes in laws, regulations, or government policies may affect the value or legality of certain investments and could impact market operations.
