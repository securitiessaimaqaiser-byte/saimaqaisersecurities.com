Introduction
Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal information of its clients, investors, and website users. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, used, stored, and protected in accordance with applicable laws of Pakistan, including regulatory requirements of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) and Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX).

Information We Collect

We may collect personal and financial information including, but not limited to:

  • Name, CNIC / NICOP, date of birth, and nationality
  • Residential and mailing address
  • Email address and contact numbers
  • Banking and financial information (as required by law)
  • Trading and transaction records
  • Information required for KYC and AML compliance

Use of Information

Personal information is collected and used for legitimate business and regulatory purposes, including:

  • Account opening and client verification
  • Compliance with KYC, AML, and CFT regulations
  • Execution and settlement of securities transactions
  • Regulatory reporting and audit requirements
  • Responding to investor queries and complaints

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. However, information may be disclosed where required:

  • To regulators such as SECP and PSX
  • To clearing companies, custodians, and authorized service providers
  • As required under applicable laws, court orders, or legal processes

Data Storage & Jurisdiction

Personal data is stored in secure systems with access restricted to authorized personnel only. Data may be hosted on secure servers located within or outside Pakistan, subject to reasonable security controls, contractual safeguards, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained for the period required under applicable laws, SECP regulations, PSX rules, and internal record retention policies. Information is securely disposed of once it is no longer required.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is completely secure.

User Rights

Subject to applicable laws, clients may request access to or correction of their personal information by contacting the Company’s Compliance Department.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such websites.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in legal or regulatory requirements. Updated versions will be posted on this website.
