Privacy Policy

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of its clients’ personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard such information.

Information Collection

We may collect personal information including, but not limited to, identification details, contact information, and financial data provided by clients during account opening or service usage.

Use of Information

Collected information is used solely for providing brokerage and related services, complying with regulatory requirements, improving service quality, and communicating with clients where necessary.

Information Protection

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect client information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Information Sharing

Client information is not disclosed to third parties except where required by law, regulatory authorities, or with the client’s consent.

Policy Updates

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
