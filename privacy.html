Introduction
Saima Qaiser Securities (Private) Limited (“the Company”, “we”, “our” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal information of its clients, investors, and website users. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is collected, processed, stored, disclosed, and protected in accordance with applicable laws of Pakistan, including the regulatory requirements of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) and Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX).

Information We Collect

We may collect personal, financial, and technical information, including but not limited to:

  • Name, CNIC / NICOP, date of birth, and nationality
  • Residential and mailing addresses
  • Email address and contact numbers
  • Banking and financial information as required by law
  • Trading, transaction, and account records
  • KYC, AML, and CFT related information
  • Website usage data such as IP address, browser type, and access logs

Legal Basis for Processing

Personal information is collected and processed on the basis of contractual necessity, legal and regulatory obligations, legitimate business purposes, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

Use of Information

  • Account opening, maintenance, and client verification
  • Compliance with KYC, AML, and CFT requirements
  • Execution, clearing, and settlement of transactions
  • Regulatory reporting, audits, and inspections
  • Responding to investor queries, complaints, or legal obligations

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be disclosed only where necessary:

  • To regulators including SECP and PSX
  • To clearing companies, custodians, banks, and authorized service providers
  • As required by applicable laws, court orders, or lawful authorities

Data Storage & Jurisdiction

Personal data is stored in secure systems with access restricted to authorized personnel only. Data may be hosted within or outside Pakistan, subject to reasonable security safeguards, contractual protections, and applicable regulatory requirements.

Data Retention

Personal information is retained for periods required under applicable laws, SECP regulations, PSX rules, and internal record retention policies. Information is securely disposed of once it is no longer legally or operationally required.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

User Rights

Subject to applicable laws and regulatory requirements, clients may request access to, correction, or updating of their personal information by contacting the Company’s Compliance Department. Certain requests may be restricted where retention is required by law.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. The Company is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of such external websites.

Changes to This Policy
