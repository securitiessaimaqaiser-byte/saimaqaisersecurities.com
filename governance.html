Corporate Governance

Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is committed to maintaining high standards of corporate governance in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and best practices prescribed by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors is responsible for providing strategic direction, oversight, and governance to ensure the long-term sustainability and regulatory compliance of the company.

  • Overall strategic guidance and policy formulation
  • Oversight of management performance
  • Ensuring compliance with SECP regulations
  • Risk management and internal controls

Details of individual directors are disclosed in accordance with regulatory requirements and are available upon request where applicable.

Share Holding Patterns

The shareholding structure of Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited reflects transparency and compliance with corporate and regulatory disclosure standards.

Shareholding information is maintained internally and disclosed to regulators as required under applicable laws and SECP directives.
