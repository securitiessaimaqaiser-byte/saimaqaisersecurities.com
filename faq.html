Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited regulated?

Yes. Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited is licensed and regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) and operates under applicable securities laws and regulations.

How can I open a brokerage account?

Investors can open an account by completing the Account Opening Form and fulfilling KYC/CDD requirements as prescribed by SECP regulations.

What documents are required for account opening?

Generally required documents include a valid CNIC, proof of address, bank account details, and other information as required under KYC/CDD regulations.

Are my investments guaranteed?

No. Investments in capital markets involve risk. Saima Qaiser Securities (Pvt) Limited does not guarantee returns or protection against losses.

How can I submit a complaint?

Complaints can be submitted through our Feedback Form or directly to SECP or Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) using their official online portals.

Where can I access financial statements?

Audited financial statements are available under the Investor Information section of this website.
